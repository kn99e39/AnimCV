## FK semantics and controlled results

For each frame and direction mapping, both source joints must be structurally valid. The solver records the raw canonical 3D direction, removes that frame's existing valid-only Root Orientation yaw, maps the heading-relative direction through the fixed rig alignment, and applies a deterministic shortest-arc swing from the imported bone's head→tail rest direction. It then removes the mapped ancestor's world delta and conjugates through the imported bone's rest basis to produce a Blender bone-**local** quaternion. The declared `spine_01` receives root yaw once. Unknown yaw, invalid endpoints, or unavailable mapped parents yield an explicit `UNAVAILABLE` sample with no quaternion; no identity/hold/interpolation is substituted. Axial twist remains unobserved and uses neutral zero-twist swing.

The synthetic 0°/45°/90° rigid-body yaw control changed the root owner rotation while preserving the upper-arm and forearm local quaternions, including when run against the extracted BaseRig snapshot. With yaw fixed, upper-arm motion changed its local quaternion and elbow bend changed the forearm local quaternion. A first frame already rotated away from rest produced a non-identity upper-arm quaternion; source frame zero did not define rest. Tests also covered arbitrary rest axis, rotated rest basis, 180° reversal, normalization/determinism, parent→child composition, invalid evidence and unknown yaw, serialization, and snapshot extraction/round-trip.

## Architecture verdict and remaining blocker

**NO for the requested whole-body BaseRig Root Orientation contract; YES for the scoped mapped upper-body FK control.** The imported armature has five independent top-level bones: `spine_01`, `thigh_l`, `thigh_r`, `interaction`, and `center_of_mass`. `spine_01` is an explicit ancestor of both mapped left-arm bones, so its yaw is inherited once and never leaks into their local channels. It is **not** an ancestor of either thigh, calf, or foot. The Blender turning review shows the torso/arm rotating while the legs remain stationary. Eight imported bones are outside the `spine_01` yaw subtree. No single existing target bone owns whole-body yaw on this imported BaseRig. Choosing a different bone per clip, adding per-bone yaw corrections, or silently changing BaseRig would violate this batch's contract. A future rig-level/object-level owner or an explicit common skeletal root contract is required before claiming whole-body FK retargeting. Stop here; do not proceed into IK.
