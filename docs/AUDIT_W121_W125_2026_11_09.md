**CRITICAL acceptance bypass (CODE + AGENT-RUN exploratory, NOT independent rerun).** `_propose` writes an unsealed `edits/<id>/proposal.json` containing the computed decision. `_commit` checks `prop["decision"]["decision"] == "ACCEPT"`, candidate content address and candidate .blend SHA, but it does **not** independently recompute the frozen W116/W118 gate on the accepted Blender readback. `_publication_checks` compares accepted .blend/readback and footprint **against values taken from that same proposal**, and computes a pin residual without testing it against the experimental 1 mm gate. A modified proposal can therefore make an infeasible pin look approved even while the copied .blend and footprint remain bit-equal to the candidate. W125's post-outcome test reports a 136 mm residual accepted with exit 0. `baseline/bindings.json` and `edits/<id>/request.json` are also not included in the W124 `immutable_files` guard, so their identity/authority must be addressed in any corrective contract. Distinguish accidental corruption detection by an unkeyed SHA from adversarial authenticity; do not claim cryptographic user authentication.

**Secondary limitations.** W125 `verify_head` checks content-addressed state and stored .blend/readback hashes, not a fresh Blender process on every `status`; fresh readback is part of the publication and Agent-run step audit.
