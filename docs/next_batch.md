## PURPOSE
The product objective is controllable, production-useful animation, not merely low 3D joint error. The user's three motivating gaps are occluded-joint unreliability, the distinction between faithful motion capture and game-production animation, and insufficient opportunities for user supervision. Before choosing a model or rewriting the intermediate representation, establish exactly which observed facts, estimated motion, uncertainty indicators, identity/time/coordinate contracts and edit/lock directives survive existing boundaries. Separate missing **data**, missing **producer**, missing **consumer**, and ambiguous **semantics**; never call an inference a measured fact.

## CENTRAL INTENT
Can the current AnimCV FramePose → intermediate-motion → rig handoff, with at most an isolated non-production read-only/scratch sidecar view, preserve and unambiguously distinguish (1) actual 2D video observations, (2) estimated 3D pose and its evidence limits, (3) joint/frame/sequence provenance and uncertainty indicators, and (4) explicit user-locked versus editable regions, such that a selective Generator/Editor could preserve trusted reference motion and modify only authorized regions—without silent ID/time/coordinate loss, confidence misinterpretation or unintended edits?

**C — PARTIAL / INDETERMINATE:** validity gates pass but the minimum contract is only partially joinable, a necessary signal has no current producer/consumer, a rig boundary remains untested, or evidence is insufficient to claim A or a demonstrated irrecoverable B. List precise missing ownership and conditions; do not convert C to A by shrinking scope after results.
Precedence D > B > A > C. Report all predeclared decision inputs and counterexamples; any failure not covered by a gate must remain visible.
