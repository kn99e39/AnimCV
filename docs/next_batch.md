# BATCH 110 — FBX-Embedded Interpolation Semantics Recovery Feasibility (Evidence Only)

## HUMAN ASSESSMENT — CURRENT JUDGMENT
**SUCCESS / SUPPORTED:** W109 is committed COMPLETE, CASE A. On Blender 5.1.0 / io_scene_fbx 5.15.0, byte-isolated changes of the selected FBX curves from LINEAR 0x6104 to valid CONSTANT-standard 0x6002 or CONSTANT-next 0x6102 were independently parsed, but the unchanged importer produced bitwise-identical LINEAR F-curves and motion. W108 had already shown that source Actions are held CONSTANT, the stock exporter serializes LINEAR, and changing the *imported* curves back to CONSTANT restores the three real Actions' quarter-frame world-motion parity to approximately 1 µm. W107 showed that disabling key simplification restores integer-frame parity, at a substantial key/file cost.
**FAILURE / NOT FIXED:** The stock Blender-to-FBX-to-Blender path still does not preserve held subframe motion; both exporter encoding and importer interpretation lose semantics. A blanket post-import CONSTANT conversion damages genuinely smooth curves (W108 smooth control 0.51 mm → 86 mm). Multi-take, frame_end 250, native 50→30 fps mismatch, and storage cost remain open.
**UNCERTAINTY:** No end-to-end *metadata-driven*, per-curve mixed-interpolation recovery has been tested. In particular, W108's correction was applied using knowledge of the source Action, not an autonomous FBX-only consumer mapping. W109's encoded flags were ignored by the stock importer, not consumed by a separate reader. Mapping FBX AnimationCurves to imported Blender F-curves may be ambiguous after Euler conversion, bone remapping or take import. W106–W109 Main-local binaries and tests were reported by the Agent, not independently replayed by this reviewer.
**ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS / LIMITS:** Source-to-FBX and FBX-to-Blender channel correspondence, hidden resampling, object versus pose channels, importer-created bones, smooth/held mixed channels, or consumer-specific semantics could prevent reliable restoration even when the flag is present. This is a Blender 5.1 feasibility question, not general Autodesk/Unreal/other-consumer interoperability.
**HUMAN DECISION:** The user explicitly chose to continue FBX compatibility recovery rather than defer to a Blender-native-only route. This authorizes a bounded *feasibility experiment*, not adoption of an exporter policy, a patched importer, a new production adapter or a downstream feature.
**WHY NOW:** W108/W109 establish two distinct failure boundaries. Before any production change, determine whether the FBX file can carry sufficient per-curve semantics for a consumer-side recovery step without access to the source .blend/Action.
